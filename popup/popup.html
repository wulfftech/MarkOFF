    What's a marketplace?

    Retailers like Big W, Bunnings and JB Hi-Fi now let other companies list products on their websites. These third-party sellers handle their own stock, shipping, and returns — but the listing looks identical to the retailer's own products.

    About 65% of Australian shoppers don't know this is happening. Which is presumably the point.

    What MarkOFF does

    • Remove mode — hides marketplace items from search and category pages, or auto-clicks the site's own filter button where one exists.
    • Highlight mode — keeps everything visible but marks marketplace items with a purple outline and label.
    • Detail page warnings — shows a banner on product pages when the seller is a third party, with a link to ACCC guidance.

    The badge

    A purple badge in the bottom-right corner shows how many items were hidden. Click it to temporarily peek at them (they appear dimmed). Click again to re-hide.

    Consumer rights

    When buying from a marketplace seller, your consumer guarantees still apply under Australian Consumer Law — but the retailer's own policies (easy returns, click & collect, price match) may not. The third-party seller is the responsible party, not the retailer.
