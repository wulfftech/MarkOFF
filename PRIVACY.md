# MarkOFF — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026

## The short version

MarkOFF collects nothing. There is no backend. There is no account. There is no analytics. Nothing you do is recorded, transmitted, or stored anywhere except your own browser.

## What the extension does

MarkOFF reads the page you're currently on to detect third-party marketplace listings, then hides or labels them depending on your settings. That's it. The page content is processed locally in your browser and goes nowhere.

## What data is stored

Your preferences — filter mode (Remove or Highlight) and per-site enable/disable toggles — are saved using `chrome.storage.sync`. This is local browser storage that syncs across your own devices via your Google account. No one else can read it. We can't read it.

## What data is transmitted

None. MarkOFF makes no network requests. There is no server. There is no telemetry. There is no crash reporting. The extension operates entirely offline.

## Third parties

MarkOFF is not affiliated with any of the retailers it filters. It does not communicate with them. It does not share data with anyone because it has no data to share.
